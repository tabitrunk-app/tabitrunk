Privacy Policy

Effective: October 9, 2026

Information we collect

The app "Trip Trunk: Packing Puzzle" (the App) does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location or device identifiers. There is no account.

Your progress (puzzles solved, stamps, sound and language settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Ads, analytics and tracking

The App uses no advertising, analytics or tracking.

In-app purchase

Payment for "Open all trips" is handled by Apple; neither the App nor the developer receives your payment details. Whether you have bought it is checked on your device from Apple's purchase records (StoreKit), and the result is not sent to the developer.

Information you send us

If you email us, your address and message are used only to reply and to look into problems.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be announced on this page. Contact: tabitrunk.app@gmail.com
