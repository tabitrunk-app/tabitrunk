How to play

Drag items into the trunk and tap to turn them. When everything fits, the puzzle is solved. Finish all ten puzzles of a trip to earn its stamp. Hints are always there when you need them.

No login or account is needed, and the game plays offline.

FAQ

What can I play without buying?

The first trip, Seaside Town (10 puzzles), and selected trial stages from later trips. Buying "Open all trips" once in the app opens all 6 included trips and 60 puzzles.

How do I restore my purchase?

With the same Apple Account, tap "Restore purchase" on the purchase screen or in Settings, also after a new phone or a reinstall.

Can I move my progress?

Progress (puzzles solved, stamps, settings) is kept only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move to another device. Your purchase can always be restored from Apple's records.
